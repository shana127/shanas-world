🍰 Baking Recipes

My favorite sweet treats to make 💖

🍪 Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients:

  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 cup butter (softened)
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • chocolate chips

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Mix butter + sugars until fluffy
  3. Add eggs + vanilla
  4. Mix dry ingredients separately, then combine
  5. Stir in chocolate chips
  6. Bake 10–12 minutes

🧁 Vanilla Cupcakes

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Mix butter + sugar
  3. Add eggs + vanilla
  4. Add dry ingredients + milk slowly
  5. Pour into cupcake liners
  6. Bake 18–20 minutes

🍫 Fudgy Brownies

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch of salt

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Melt butter and mix with sugar
  3. Add eggs + vanilla
  4. Mix in cocoa, flour, salt
  5. Pour into pan
  6. Bake 20–25 minutes
