🍰 Baking Recipes

My Pinterest-style sweet treats collection 💖

🍪 Cookies

⏱ 30 min | ⭐ Easy

Ingredients:

  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Chocolate chips
  • Eggs

Steps:

  1. Mix butter + sugar
  2. Add eggs + vanilla
  3. Add dry ingredients
  4. Add chocolate chips
  5. Bake 350°F for 10–12 min

🧁 Cupcakes

⏱ 45 min | ⭐ Medium

Ingredients:

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla

Steps:

  1. Mix wet ingredients
  2. Add dry ingredients
  3. Pour into liners
  4. Bake 18–20 min

🍫 Brownies

⏱ 40 min | ⭐ Easy

Ingredients:

  • Cocoa powder
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Flour
  • Eggs

Steps:

  1. Melt butter
  2. Mix with sugar + eggs
  3. Add cocoa + flour
  4. Bake 350°F for 20–25 min
